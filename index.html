About

You’ve mastered the rules and built your lore—now give your tabletop forces the presence they deserve. Sir Oliver’s Customs offers an all-in-one commission studio designed to take your unique ideas and turn them into physical reality.
We don't just paint miniatures; we help create them. Whether you have a completely unique character design that needs to be brought to life from scratch, or a massive wargaming army sitting in a "pile of shame," we handle the entire creative pipeline. From initial custom design concepts to meticulous, high-quality hand-painting, we deliver striking, cohesive figures engineered to stand out on any battlefield or display shelf.
Your lore isn't generic. Your miniatures shouldn't be either.
