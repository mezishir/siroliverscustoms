SERVICE AGREEMENT & TERMS

Please note these terms must be agreed upon before work can commence.

Scope of Work & Artistic Liberty

The Artist agrees to perform miniature painting services as described in the project quote. By commissioning the Artist, the Client acknowledges that they are purchasing a bespoke artistic service. Hand-painted items will naturally have minor variations and artistic nuances.

Style Acceptance: The Client confirms they have reviewed the Artist’s portfolio and accept the Artist’s unique style, technique, and creative interpretation as the standard for the project.

Payments & Retainers

Non-Refundable Retainer: A 50% deposit is required to secure a slot in the commission schedule. This fee covers administrative costs, materials preparation, and the reservation of the Artist’s time. It is strictly non-refundable.

Final Balance: The remaining 50% balance, plus shipping costs, is due upon completion and approval of final high-resolution photographs.

Late Payments: Work will not be shipped until the final balance has cleared.

The Milestone Approval Process

To ensure total transparency and protect both parties from disputes regarding "flaws" or "quality," the following workflow is mandatory: "Once the Client provides written approval of the final project photos via email or website message, the service is deemed 'Accepted as Described.' No further modifications will be made, and no refunds will be issued based on aesthetic critiques after this point." If the Client identifies a legitimate deviation from the original agreed-upon brief during the photo review stage, the Artist will correct the specific issue at no extra charge before shipping.



Refund & Cancellation Policy

Custom Nature of Work: Due to the irreversible nature of labor and material application, refunds are not offered once work has commenced. Kill Fee: If a project is cancelled by the Client after the retainer is paid but before completion, the Artist reserves the right to retain the deposit and charge a 'Kill Fee' proportional to the percentage of work completed.

Shipping & Liability

The Artist will package all miniatures with extreme care (foam, bubble wrap, or specialized cases). However, the Artist is not responsible for damages incurred during transit by third-party couriers. It is highly recommended that Clients request insured shipping for large or expensive armies.

Dispute Resolution

In the event of a payment dispute or chargeback attempt, this signed agreement and the documented "Final Approval" communication will be submitted as evidence of service fulfillment.
